Detail 2

The short version, before any images

Conversational Insurance Flow

The problem

A car insurance quote asked people 30 questions before giving them anything, in one long form filled in by typing.

What I did

Rebuilt it as a conversation, one clear question at a time, with every answer editable and consent asked where it's needed.

What changed

23 form fields became 19 questions across 5 named stages, answered by tapping instead of typing.
